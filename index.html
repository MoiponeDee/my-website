About Bontle Digital

Bontle Digital is a Pretoria-based web and app development company serving businesses across South Africa. We build websites, web apps and mobile apps that look professional, load quickly and help customers get in touch or buy.

The name comes from the Setswana word for “beautiful”, which is the standard we hold every project to: well designed, well built and easy to use. You work directly with the developer who builds your project, so there are no account managers, no hand-offs and no surprises on the price.
